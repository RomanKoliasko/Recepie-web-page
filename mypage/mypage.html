TRADITIONAL UKRAINIAN BORSCH

Ukrainian Borscht is more than just a soup—it’s a timeless symbol of tradition and comfort. Made with beets, hearty vegetables, and aromatic spices, it offers a unique blend of sweet, sour, and savory flavors. Enjoy it piping hot with sour cream for the ultimate taste of Ukraine.

Preparation time

• Total: Approximately 2 hours

• Preparation: Approximately 30 minutes

• Cooking: Approximately 1 hour 30 minutes

Ingredients

• 500g pork or beef (on the bone)

• 2 large beets (grated)

• 3-4 potatoes (cubed)

• 1/2 head of cabbage (shredded)

• 1 large carrot (grated)

• 1 onion (diced)

• 2 tbsp tomato paste

• 3 cloves garlic (minced)

• Fresh dill and parsley

• Sour cream (for serving)

Spices

• 2 bay leaves

• 5 black peppercorns

• 1 tbsp sugar

• 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar

• Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

1. Make the Broth

Place the meat in a large pot, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Add bay leaves and peppercorns, then simmer on low heat for about 1 hour until tender.

2. Sauté the Vegetables (Zazharka)

Heat oil in a pan, sauté diced onion and grated carrot until soft. Stir in grated beets, tomato paste, sugar, and lemon juice (or vinegar). Cook for 10–12 minutes on medium heat.

3. Cook the Base

Remove the cooked meat from the broth, dice it, and return it to the pot. Add cubed potatoes and shredded cabbage, then simmer for 15 minutes until soft.

4. Combine & Season

Add the sautéed vegetable mixture into the pot. Season with salt, black pepper, and minced garlic. Stir well and simmer for another 5 minutes.

5. Rest & Serve

Turn off the heat, cover with a lid, and let it rest for 15 minutes. Serve hot with a dollop of sour cream and fresh dill or parsley.

CHEF’S TIPS

• Keep the Color: Add a splash of lemon juice or vinegar to the beets while cooking to preserve their vibrant red color.

• The Perfect Pairing: Always serve hot with a dollop of full-fat sour cream and fresh, warm Pampushky (garlic bread).

• Let it Rest: For the best flavor, let the borscht rest for 15–20 minutes before serving.

• Broth Flavor: Use bone-in meat for a richer, more flavorful broth.
